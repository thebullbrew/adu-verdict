The method

Yield on cost is the tell. NOI divided by total project cost. If a finished rental down the street yields 5.5% and your ADU yields 4%, you built an expensive way to buy income. Above roughly 6.5%, building starts to beat buying.

Value added is not project cost. Spending $235,000 does not add $235,000 to your appraisal. This tool keeps those numbers separate on purpose — that gap is where most ADU pitches lie to you.

Check the rules before the math matters: zoning and lot coverage, owner-occupancy rules, separate metering, parking, short-term-rental bans, and whether your town reassesses the whole property. No spreadsheet survives an illegal unit.

All math runs in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Scenarios save only on this device. This is an underwriting aid, not financial, legal, or tax advice — verify zoning, permits, and loan terms locally before you build.
